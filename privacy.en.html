Privacy Policy — Tafawt

Last updated: May 2026

Welcome to Tafawt, an Amazigh language learning application. This policy clearly and transparently explains what data is collected and how it is used.

1. Data Collected

Tafawt works without account creation and does not collect any personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number, etc.).

The only data processed are:

  • Local progress — your progress through levels, gems, hearts, and daily streak are stored only on your device via iOS UserDefaults. This data never leaves your phone.
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) — used by our advertising partner Google AdMob to display ads. You can refuse this tracking at any time via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt at first launch, or in iOS Settings > Privacy > Tracking.

2. Advertising

Tafawt occasionally displays ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • Interstitial ads between certain levels (rare and limited)
  • Rewarded ads on explicit request (recovering hearts, energy)

These ads may collect technical information (device model, iOS version, advertising identifier) for measurement and personalization purposes. For more information, see Google's privacy policy.

3. In-App Purchases

Tafawt offers in-app purchases managed exclusively by Apple via StoreKit:

  • Support donations ($1.99 / $4.99 / $9.99)
  • Bonus level unlocks paid with gems

No payment information passes through Tafawt. Your credit card and financial data remain managed by Apple.

4. Pronunciation and Audio

To generate Kabyle word pronunciation, Tafawt uses the ElevenLabs service via an anonymous request. No personal data is transmitted — only the text of the word to pronounce.

5. Content Reporting

If you use the "Report a problem" feature, your message is sent via standard email from your iOS Mail app. Tafawt does not store the content of these reports.

6. Your Rights

You can at any time:

  • Reset all your progress in the app settings
  • Uninstall the app — all local data is then deleted
  • Refuse advertising tracking via iOS Settings
  • Contact us with any questions: flowcore.digital@gmail.com

7. Children

Tafawt is suitable for use by all, including accompanied children. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from minors.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of the page indicates the last modification. Any significant changes will be signaled within the application.

9. Contact

For any privacy questions or to exercise your rights:
