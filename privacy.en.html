Privacy Policy — Amazia

Last updated: September 2026

Amazia is an app for learning the Amazigh languages. This policy explains what data is handled, and how.

1. Data we collect

Amazia works without an account and collects no personally identifiable information: no name, no email address, no phone number.

  • Progress — levels completed, vocabulary seen, gems, energy, daily streak. This data is stored on your device only and is never transmitted.
  • Advertising identifier — used by Google AdMob to serve ads. You can refuse it at any time, either in the prompt shown at launch or in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

2. Microphone recordings

Pronunciation exercises let you record yourself and compare your voice with the speaker's.

These recordings never leave your device. They are not transmitted, not analysed by any third party, and not kept after the exercise. iOS asks for microphone permission, and the app works without it: pronunciation levels remain available to listen to, simply without the option to record.

3. Advertising

Amazia shows ads served by Google AdMob:

  • interstitial ads, between some levels;
  • rewarded ads, only when you ask for one yourself to recover energy or hearts.

These ads may collect technical information: device model, iOS version, advertising identifier. See Google's privacy policy.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent form appears on first launch and lets you accept or refuse personalised advertising. You can change your choice at any time from the app's settings. Refusing does not prevent you from using Amazia.

People who have purchased Amazia Premium see no ads at all.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through StoreKit. No payment details ever pass through Amazia.

  • unlocking bonus levels and mastery checkpoints;
  • Amazia Premium: all content, no ads, unlimited energy;
  • gems, the app's internal currency;
  • support donations, with nothing given in return.

5. Reporting content

The "Report a problem" feature opens your Mail app with a pre-filled message. Nothing is sent without your confirmation, and Amazia keeps no record of reports.

6. Your rights

  • reset all your progress from the app's settings;
  • uninstall the app, which deletes all local data;
  • refuse or withdraw ad tracking, and change your consent to personalised advertising;
  • contact us: flowcore.digital@gmail.com.

7. Children

Amazia is suitable for all audiences and knowingly collects no personally identifiable information from minors. The app is not specifically directed at children within the meaning of app store rules.

8. Changes

This policy may change. The date at the top of the page shows the most recent update.

9. Contact
