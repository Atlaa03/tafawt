Purchases

I changed phones and lost my purchase.

Your purchases belong to your Apple account, not to your device. Open the Premium tab and tap Restore purchases. First make sure you are signed in with the same Apple account that paid.

What is the difference between "All levels" and "Full Premium"?

"All levels" unlocks bonus levels and mastery checkpoints. "Full Premium" adds all the culture content, the training cycles and unlimited energy, and removes every ad. Both are one-time purchases, not subscriptions.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Energy and gems

I've run out of energy.

It refills every day. In the meantime you can refill it with gems, or watch an ad to get one back. With Premium it is unlimited.

What are gems for?

The shop: refilling energy, getting hearts back, protecting your streak. You earn them by completing levels and challenges.

Pronunciation

The microphone doesn't work.

Allow it in iOS Settings › Amazia › Microphone. Your recordings stay on your phone and are never sent anywhere.

A word has no sound.

Every word is recorded by a native speaker, not a synthetic voice. Some words have not been recorded yet — they will be added in future updates.

Content

A word looks wrong, or we say it differently where I'm from.

The Amazigh languages vary a great deal from region to region. Use Report a problem in the app, or write to me with your region: this feedback is how new variants get added.

Which languages are available?

Kabyle and Central Atlas Tamazight. Tarifit, Tachelhit and Chaoui are in preparation.

Privacy

How do I change my ad choices?

In the app's settings, under Ad preferences. This option only appears in countries covered by European regulations. You can also refuse tracking in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

How do I erase my progress?

In the app's settings, with the reset button. Uninstalling the app also deletes all your data.
